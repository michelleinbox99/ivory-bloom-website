Accessibility at the Atelier

Every bride deserves to plan her day here comfortably. We build this site toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do. Full keyboard navigation (the cart drawer traps focus and closes on Escape), visible focus indicators, labeled form fields with clear inline errors, descriptive alt text on photography, ARIA labels on icon buttons, touch targets of at least 44px, reduced-motion support for animations, and text set against our palette at readable contrast.

Known limits. Some editorial photographs contain decorative text; we keep meaningful content in real text. If you find anything else, we want to know.

Tell us. If any part of the site is hard to use with your assistive technology, write to michelleinbox99@gmail.com and we will fix it or personally help you complete your order by email — whichever is faster for you.
